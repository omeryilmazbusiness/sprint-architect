Support

Last updated: 28 May 2026

Healory helps operations teams manage customers, schedules, documents, and day-to-day workflows. If you need help using the mobile app or have questions about your account, contact us using the details below.

Contact

  • Email: support@healory.app

We aim to respond within 2 business days. For urgent matters related to your active plan, please contact your organization directly.

Who can use Healory?

Access is provided by your organization. Team members receive email and password credentials. If you were given an access key, please contact your administrator for help.

Common topics

  • Sign-in issues — Confirm your email/password or access key with your organization administrator.
  • Notifications — Enable notifications in iOS Settings → Healory.
  • Documents — Your organization assigns document types; you can upload PDFs only for assigned types under the Files tab. Enable camera/photo or file access in iOS Settings if prompted.
  • Data after your visit — Guest data is automatically removed from the platform after your departure date. You can also use Profile → Delete my data to schedule removal sooner.
  • Account deletion — Use in-app self-delete (Profile) or contact your organization administrator. Staff accounts: email support@healory.app.
