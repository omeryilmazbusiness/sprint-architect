Support

Last updated: 21 May 2026

Healory helps health tourism clinics manage guests, appointments, documents, and day-to-day operations. If you need help using the mobile app or have questions about your account, contact us using the details below.

Contact

  • Email: support@healory.app

We aim to respond within 2 business days. For urgent clinical matters, please contact your clinic directly.

Who can use Healory?

Access is provided by your clinic or organization. Clinic staff receive email and password credentials. Guests may sign in with a patient key supplied by their clinic.

Common topics

  • Sign-in issues — Confirm your email/password or patient key with your clinic administrator.
  • Notifications — Enable notifications in iOS Settings → Healory.
  • Documents — Upload problems are usually resolved by checking camera/photo permissions in Settings.
  • Account deletion — Request removal through your clinic or email us at support@healory.app.
