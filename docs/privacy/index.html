Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Healory (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Healory mobile application and related services (the “Service”). The Service is intended for health tourism clinics and their guests/patients.

1. Who we are

Healory provides a platform for clinic operations and guest journey management. Your clinic is the primary organization responsible for your care; we process data on behalf of clinic workflows you participate in.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information — Email address, name, role (e.g. clinic staff or guest), and clinic association.
  • Authentication data — Credentials or patient keys used to sign in; tokens stored securely on your device where applicable.
  • Operational & health journey data — Appointments, treatment plans, documents you or your clinic upload, transport and hotel details, and related metadata provided by your clinic.
  • Device & push tokens — Push notification tokens and platform identifiers to deliver alerts about appointments and documents.
  • Technical logs — Limited server logs (e.g. request time, error codes) for security and reliability; we do not sell this data.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the Service for your clinic and your account
  • Send in-app and push notifications about appointments, documents, and status updates
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve reliability
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable

4. Legal basis & clinic responsibility

Clinics instruct guests and staff to use Healory. Clinics are responsible for the accuracy of medical and personal data they enter. If you have questions about your treatment records, contact your clinic first.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only:

  • With your clinic and authorized clinic users as part of the Service
  • With infrastructure providers (e.g. cloud hosting, storage, email) under contractual safeguards
  • When required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

6. Storage & security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Production environments use industry-standard access controls, encrypted connections, and secure credential storage practices. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we work to protect data proportionate to its sensitivity.

7. Retention

We retain information while your account is active and as needed for clinic operations, legal compliance, and dispute resolution. Deletion requests should be directed to your clinic or to privacy@healory.app.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. Contact us or your clinic to exercise these rights.

9. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13. Clinics must not create accounts for children without appropriate consent and legal compliance.

10. International transfers

Data may be processed in countries where our servers or subprocessors operate. We apply appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers where required.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

  • Privacy: privacy@healory.app
  • Support: Support page or support@healory.app
