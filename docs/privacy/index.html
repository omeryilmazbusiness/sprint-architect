Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Healory (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Healory mobile application and related services (the “Service”). The Service is intended for organizations and their teams and customers.

1. Who we are

Healory provides a platform for operational workflows. Your organization is responsible for how it uses the Service; we process data on behalf of the workflows you participate in.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information — Email address, name, role (e.g. team member or end user), and organization association.
  • Authentication data — Credentials or guest access keys used to sign in; tokens stored securely on your device where applicable.
  • Operational data — Schedules, plans, visit details, and PDF files you or your organization upload for assigned operational document types (e.g. travel or identity paperwork requested by your organization), and related metadata.
  • Device & push tokens — Push notification tokens and platform identifiers to deliver alerts.
  • Technical logs — Limited server logs (e.g. request time, error codes) for security and reliability; we do not sell this data.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the Service for your organization and your account
  • Send in-app and push notifications about activity and status updates
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve reliability
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable

4. Legal basis & organization responsibility

Organizations instruct users to use Healory. Organizations are responsible for the accuracy of the data they enter. If you have questions about records created by your organization, contact your administrator first.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only:

  • With your organization and authorized users as part of the Service
  • With infrastructure providers (e.g. cloud hosting, storage, email) under contractual safeguards
  • When required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

6. Storage & security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Production environments use industry-standard access controls, encrypted connections, and secure credential storage practices. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we work to protect data proportionate to its sensitivity.

7. Retention & deletion

Guest (end-user) accounts. Operational data linked to a guest profile — including profile details, plan, visits, device binding, and uploaded PDF files — is removed from the platform when the guest’s departure date ends (based on the calendar day in the institution’s configured timezone, default Europe/Istanbul). Guests may also request earlier removal in the app under Profile → Delete my data, after which data is scheduled for removal within a short grace period.

Institution staff accounts. We retain staff and organization data while the subscription is active and as needed for billing, security, and legal compliance.

Operational exports. Before scheduled guest data removal, your organization’s designated manager may receive an automated operational summary (PDF and copies of uploaded PDFs) for internal record-keeping. These exports are for operations only and are not medical records.

Billing-related counters (e.g. approved guest counts per billing period) may be retained without personal identifiers where required for invoicing. For other deletion requests, contact your organization administrator or privacy@healory.app.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. Contact us or your organization to exercise these rights.

9. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13. Organizations must not create accounts for children without appropriate consent and legal compliance.

10. International transfers

Data may be processed in countries where our servers or subprocessors operate. We apply appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers where required.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

  • Support: Support page or support@healory.app
