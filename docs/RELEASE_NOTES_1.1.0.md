## App Description (English) — if updating full description

Healory helps organizations manage guest journeys end to end: schedules, transport, hotels, documents, and team workflows. Staff use secure email sign-in; guests use a personal access key. Guest data is tied to travel dates and can be removed after departure or on request from Profile.

**For guests:** view your plan, appointments, assigned files, and contact your organization.  
**For managers:** members, services, providers, invoices, and operational tools in one app.  
**For administrators:** organizations, users, billing, and platform settings.
