## 6. Description (App Store — İngilizce, yapıştır)

```
Healory helps communities and event organizers stay organized together.

Whether you run a travel group, retreat, fan community, or multi-location team, Healory gives hosts simple tools to plan events, share updates, and collect member uploads—while members follow their personal timeline and scheduled activities.

FOR COMMUNITY HOSTS
• Manage members and invite codes
• Build event schedules and timelines
• Request and review member uploads (photos, PDFs)
• In-app notifications for activity updates

FOR MEMBERS (INVITE-ONLY)
• Sign in with a private invite code from your host
• View your timeline and upcoming events
• Upload files your community requested
• Manage your profile and notification preferences

PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Invite-only member access
• Secure host sign-in
• Member data handling described in our Privacy Policy

Healory is coordination software for groups—it does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, or clinical care, and it is not a marketplace for regulated professional services.

Questions? Visit our Support page or contact us through App Store Connect.
```
