Thank you for your message. Below are detailed answers. **Healory does not offer highly regulated services** (no medical diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, or clinical care through the app). It is a **community and events coordination app** for invited members and community hosts.

---

## Question: Which companies or institutions provide the services offered in the app?

**None through the app.** Healory is software that independent communities and event organizers use to share schedules, timelines, and member-uploaded media (e.g. photos, PDFs). The app does not deliver medical, legal, financial, or other regulated professional services and does not list or endorse specific service providers.

---

## Question: What is the relationship between Omer Yilmaz and the providers of these services?

**Omer Yilmaz is the individual developer and publisher of the Healory app only.** He is not a healthcare provider, clinic, or employer of any organization shown in the app. Communities that choose to use Healory operate independently and are responsible for their own content and offline activities.

---

## Question: Who are the intended users that can log in using Guest / Member accounts?

**Member accounts** are for **invited participants** who receive an **invite code** from a community host (organizer). Members can:

- View their personal timeline and scheduled **events**
- Upload files/media requested by the host (e.g. profile photo, travel PDF)
- Read in-app notifications and manage profile settings

Members **cannot** create communities, manage other users, or access host/admin tools.

---

## Question: What type of organizations is the app intended for?

Groups that run **coordinated community experiences and events** — for example travel groups, multi-location teams, retreats, fan communities, or event organizers. The app is positioned as **social / community coordination**, not as a regulated-services marketplace.
