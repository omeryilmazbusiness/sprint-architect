# App Store Connect — Guideline 2.1 answers (review build)

Use this message to reply in **Resolution Center** when Apple asks for additional information about “highly regulated services” and Guest access.

## 1) Which companies or institutions provide the services offered in the app?

No companies provide regulated services **through the app**. The app is a coordination and community experience tool (timeline/events/media) used by independent organizations to communicate schedules and collect user-submitted uploads.

The app does **not** provide medical diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, or clinical records. It does **not** connect users to regulated service providers and does not represent itself as a provider of such services.

## 2) What is the relationship between Omer Yilmaz and the providers of these services?

Omer Yilmaz is the developer/publisher of the software. Omer Yilmaz is **not** a provider of regulated services and does not employ or manage service providers through the app.

Organizations that use the app operate independently and are responsible for their own operations and content.

## 3) Who are the intended users that can login using Guest accounts?

Guest accounts are intended for **invited participants** who receive an invite code from an organization/community.

Guest users can:
- view their timeline and scheduled events
- upload permitted files/media requested by the community (e.g. profile photo, itinerary PDF)
- view notifications and profile settings

Guest users cannot administer organizations or manage other users.

## 4) What type of organizations is the app intended for?

The app is intended for organizations that run coordinated community experiences and events (e.g. multi-location teams, event organizers, communities). The app is an operations/community tool, not a regulated-services delivery app.
